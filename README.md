# Xena's Flavor Lab

### The Art & Science of Flavor

A modern culinary portfolio and hospitality website
showcasing private dining, event catering, culinary
creativity, and personalized dining experiences
in New York City.

## Live Website

[www.xenasflavorlab.com](https://www.xenasflavorlab.com/)

## About the Project

Xena's Flavor Lab brings together culinary craftsmanship,
scientific curiosity, creativity, and the art of hospitality.

The website is designed to reflect a refined yet personal
approach to food and events, combining elegant typography,
visual storytelling, and a seamless client experience.

The brand is inspired by Xena, my Scottish Fold cat,
and my passion for exploring the science behind cooking.

## Features

- Responsive and mobile-friendly design
- Elegant, editorial-inspired interface
- Private chef and event catering showcase
- Culinary photography and visual journal
- Event inquiry form with email integration
- Custom typography and brand identity
- SEO and Open Graph metadata
- Custom domain and HTTPS support

## Design Philosophy

The visual identity is built around simplicity,
elegance, and attention to detail.

- Warm cream backgrounds
- Charcoal typography
- Gold accents
- Editorial serif typography
- Handwritten typography
- Culinary photography
- Intentional use of negative space
